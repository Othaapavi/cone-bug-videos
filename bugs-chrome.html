1Scheduled date saved one day earlyhigh
PB.7 / K.26 · Chrome · 1366x768 · Fail (2/2)

What is wrong: A client request scheduled for Nov 16 3:10 PM is stored and shown as Nov 15 3:10 PM — one calendar day earlier than picked.

Why it is a bug: The firm sees and the system uses the wrong day; a date that lands in the past can silently change when or whether the request email fires.

Technical detail: Update drawer scheduleDate field reads 'Nov 15, 2026' after picking Nov 16 in the calendar popup; schedule time kept 03:10 PM.

  1. Log in to Cone.
  2. In a project, Create task > Client request > Ask a question > Continue.
  3. Set due date Nov 16; choose Schedule for later.
  4. Pick Nov 16 and 3:10 PM, click Schedule.
  5. Reopen the request and read Schedule date.
Schedule date stays Nov 16 3:10 PM.Schedule date displays Nov 15 3:10 PM — one day before what was picked.
2Schedule-for-later defaults past the due datemedium
PB.10 / K.29 · Chrome · 1366x768 · Fail (2/2)

What is wrong: Choosing Schedule for later pre-fills the next day at 02:30 AM, which is later than the request's own due date.

Why it is a bug: A request due Nov 16 defaults to being emailed Nov 17 — the client is asked for something after it is already due, and nothing warns the firm.

Technical detail: scheduleDate defaults to tomorrow; schedule time always 02:30 AM; no validation against dueDate.

  1. Log in to Cone.
  2. Create task > Client request > any type.
  3. Set Due date to today.
  4. Click Schedule for later and read the prefilled date/time.
Default schedule is before the due date, or a warning appears.Defaults to tomorrow 02:30 AM — after the due date — with no warning.

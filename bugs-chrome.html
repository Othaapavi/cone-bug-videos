1Scheduled date saved one day earlymedium
PB.7 / K.26 · Chrome · 1366x768 · Fail (2/2)

What is wrong: The schedule date shown in a sent request is one day earlier than the date picked (Nov 15 shown for a Nov 16 pick).

Why it is a bug: The firm is shown the wrong send date — it reads as already past — even though the backend still fires the email at the correct time.

Technical detail: Display-only bug: scheduleDate field reads Nov 15 after picking Nov 16; the actual scheduled email still fired on time (verified in maildrop at ~the real slot).

  1. Log in to Cone.
  2. In a project, Create task > Client request > Ask a question > Continue.
  3. Set due date Nov 16; choose Schedule for later.
  4. Pick Nov 16 and 3:10 PM, click Schedule.
  5. Reopen the request and read Schedule date.
Schedule date stays Nov 16 3:10 PM.Schedule date displays Nov 15 3:10 PM — one day before what was picked.
2Schedule-for-later defaults past the due datemedium
PB.10 / K.29 · Chrome · 1366x768 · Fail (2/2)

What is wrong: Choosing Schedule for later pre-fills the next day at 02:30 AM, which is later than the request's own due date.

Why it is a bug: A request due Nov 16 defaults to being emailed Nov 17 — the client is asked for something after it is already due, and nothing warns the firm.

Technical detail: scheduleDate defaults to tomorrow; schedule time always 02:30 AM; no validation against dueDate.

  1. Log in to Cone.
  2. Create task > Client request > any type.
  3. Set Due date to today.
  4. Click Schedule for later and read the prefilled date/time.
Default schedule is before the due date, or a warning appears.Defaults to tomorrow 02:30 AM — after the due date — with no warning.

5Can't log time on a completed taskmedium
PB.16 row 68 · Chrome · 1366x768 · Fail (2/2)

What is wrong: Completed tasks do not appear in the Add time entry task picker, so no time can ever be logged on them.

Why it is a bug: Time must often be logged after work is finished (forgotten timer). Hiding completed tasks makes that impossible; open and completed tasks should both be selectable.

Technical detail: Picker search 'DESC' returns only open 'S1 A1 desconly'; 'EMPTY' returns 'No result found' though 'S1 req EMPTY' and 'S1 req DESC' (Completed) exist in S1 alpha one-time.

  1. Log in to Cone.
  2. Work > Time entries > Add time entry.
  3. Open the task picker and search a completed task's name (e.g. 'DESC' or 'EMPTY').
Completed tasks appear in the picker and can be selected for a manual entry.Completed tasks never appear; exact-name search returns 'No result found'.
